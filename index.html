Auditory Racer

Designed for a blind player using iPad VoiceOver and touch gestures.

Welcome to Auditory Racer. Choose Start Race to begin.
Speed: 0 · Distance: 0 of 5 checkpoints

Race controls

With VoiceOver on: swipe to explore a button, then double-tap to activate it. Controls are standard buttons.

Drive

Steering is a single action. Accelerate toggles on or off so it works with VoiceOver double-tap. Brake slows the car.

Sound and help

Prototype note: best tested in Safari on the iPad with VoiceOver enabled and the device volume turned up. Speech and sound depend on browser/device settings.
